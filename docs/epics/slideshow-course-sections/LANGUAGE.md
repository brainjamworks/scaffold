# Course Sections Language

## Terms

**Course Section**
A stable, named boundary followed by a contiguous run of Slideshow Surfaces in one artefact.
Avoid: unqualified “Section”, module, chapter, lesson, unit, or SCO.

**Section boundary**
The authored structural point at which a Course Section begins.

**Member slide**
A Slideshow Surface belonging to a Course Section through the ordered boundary structure.

**Unsectioned Slideshow**
A Slideshow with no Course Section boundaries; its direct Surfaces retain ordinary linear
behaviour.

**Layout Section**
A layout-owned content container inside a Surface. It is distinct from a Course Section.

## Relationships

- A **Course Section** groups member **Surfaces** but does not contain them as persisted child
  content.
- A **Course Section** belongs to one Slideshow artefact and is not an independently persisted
  artefact.
- A **Course Section** is a structural input to navigation and future policy or delivery
  projections, not itself a completion, score, policy, launch or SCO object.

## Flagged Ambiguities

- “Section” was used for both course boundaries and layout containers. Prefer **Course Section**
  and **Layout Section** explicitly.
